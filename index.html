Search a Gurmat word or concept — meaning, root breakdown, and the tradition/language it comes from.

This is a growing starter dictionary. Entries are a best-effort synthesis drawn from multiple traditions (Sanskrit, Persian/Arabic, Sant Bhasha, Nath-yogic vocabulary) and should be cross-checked against primary sources (Mahan Kosh, Guru Granth Kosh, and the bani itself) for scriptural precision. "Scholar & Parchar Perspectives" entries are general, unverified paraphrases of a teacher's broadly known positions, not verbatim quotations — confirm specific wording against their own lectures/writings before citing them. Entries with a Grammar/Attested Senses/Operational Reading section were built without live access to a Gurbani corpus (SikhiToTheMax, SriGranth, BaniDB): a verse marked unverified was recalled from memory, not looked up, and any field left blank means it could not be recalled with confidence rather than being invented. "Operational Reading" sections are explicitly one interpretive lens, not the attested meaning — treat them accordingly.
